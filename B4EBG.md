## Before everything begins...

The worldline number of the Ellenvia was randomly comes into my mind. About months ago, when having a chemistry class, my teacher was angry about the students who made mistakes again at the question he had solved for times in his class, so he asked,"你们这点问2、4、6、7题的，我就想问我上课讲这块的时候你们在哪个世界线呢？"

Then I write down "the 4th worldline by IaSoC" on a draft. That's the origin of the official name of the LAB-E-P14-ELLE.

> By the way, despite worldline4 is the official codename of the project, we still use Ellenvia as the name in daily use. Mention it using this name.
